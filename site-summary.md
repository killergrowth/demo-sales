## What the Business Does

Demo Distributors Inc. is an authorized Anheuser-Busch beverage distributor based in El Dorado, Kansas. Founded in 1978, they distribute beer, spirits, wine, ready-to-drink beverages (RTDs), hard seltzers, and non-alcoholic options to retail and hospitality accounts. Their portfolio includes the full AB brand lineup plus craft beer and other curated brands — 100+ labels total.

## Core Services

Wholesale beverage distribution to licensed retail accounts. Primary categories: beer (AB brands + craft), spirits, wine, seltzers, RTDs, and non-alcoholic beverages.

## Target Audience

B2B only. Customers are licensed beverage retailers — liquor stores, restaurants, and bars. No direct-to-consumer sales. The "Become a Partner" CTA confirms the site is designed to convert new wholesale accounts.

## Service Area

Butler County, Kansas + the Independence territory. Regional footprint, not statewide despite "Kansas's Premier Beverage Distributor" positioning.

## Brand Tone & Voice

Professional, straightforward, and heritage-forward. The 45+ years in business and "Established 1978" messaging anchor the brand in reliability and longevity. No flashy or edgy language — this is a B2B operation that leads with trust and track record.

## Key Differentiators

- Authorized AB distributor (exclusive credentialing)
- 45+ years serving the Kansas market
- Broad multi-category portfolio (beer, spirits, wine, RTDs, NA)
- Local/regional presence with dedicated territory coverage
