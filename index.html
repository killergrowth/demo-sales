Delivering Quality Beverages Across Kansas

Demo Distributors Inc. has served the Kansas beverage market for over four decades. As an authorized Anheuser-Busch distributor, we bring the full portfolio of AB brands — plus craft beer, seltzers, RTDs, spirits, wine, and non-alcoholic options — to retailers across Butler County and Independence.

We partner with liquor stores, restaurants, and bars to ensure the right products are on the shelves, on time, every time.
