About Demo Distributors Inc.

Our History

Built on Trust,
Delivered on Time

Beer · Spirits · Wine · Food

Demo Distributors Inc. was founded in 1978 in El Dorado, Kansas by the Demo family. What started as a single authorized territory has grown into a multi-generational operation covering Butler County and the Independence territory — built on handshakes, early mornings, and a relentless commitment to keeping Kansas retailers stocked.

As an authorized Anheuser-Busch distributor, we carry the world's most recognized beer brands — and we've earned the kind of supplier relationship that comes with decades of trust. When Budweiser celebrated its 150th anniversary, our team was there. That's what a real partnership looks like.

We've also moved with the market. Today our portfolio includes Cutwater Spirits RTDs, 1PHORM Energy, craft beers, hard seltzers, wine, and non-alcoholic options — because what your customers want in 2025 isn't what they wanted in 1985, and we're not standing still.

Community

Invested in the Communities We Serve

Demo Distributors doesn't just deliver to Butler County — we're part of it. We partner with Anheuser-Busch on community initiatives like emergency water donations to local fire departments, and we show up at local events as more than just a supplier name on a banner.

We also run sampling events and trade tastings that help our retail partners introduce new products to their customers. If there's a new RTD, energy drink, or seasonal release in our portfolio — we put in the work to help it sell.
