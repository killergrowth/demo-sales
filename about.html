About Demo Sales Inc.

Kansas's trusted beverage distributor for over 45 years

Our History

Built on Trust,
Delivered on Time

Demo Sales Inc. was founded in 1978 in El Dorado, Kansas. For over four decades, we've been the backbone of beverage distribution in Butler County and the Independence territory — building relationships with retailers, staying ahead of market trends, and delivering the products customers want.

As an authorized Anheuser-Busch distributor, we represent the world's most recognized beer brands while also expanding our portfolio to include craft beers, hard seltzers, RTDs, and non-alcoholic options to meet evolving consumer demand.

Our mission is simple: be the most reliable, responsive distribution partner in Kansas. From our home base in El Dorado, we serve every corner of our territory with professionalism and deep local knowledge.
